How your algorithm works

Every article is scored across three signals: recency (newer = higher), profile match (how closely it relates to your occupation, location, and life stage), and topic relevance (keyword match strength). Your 👍 / 👎 feedback adjusts topic weights over time — the more you use it, the sharper it gets.
